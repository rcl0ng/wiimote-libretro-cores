For original, unpatched games. GunCon patches can change the required type.
Choose the Wiimote IR version of the listed device in this custom core.

GUNCON CONFIRMED; SECOND-GUN-STANDARD REPORTS DIFFER
  Mighty Hits Special: GunCon is a safe first choice. Sources differ on
  whether Justifier is also supported; verify against your specific release.

CHECK BEFORE ASSIGNING
  Simple 1500 Series Vol.024 - The Gun Shooting
  Simple 1500 Series Vol.063 - The Gun Shooting 2
  These are lightgun titles, but the primary technical list used here does
  not identify their protocol. Leave classification unconfirmed for now.
  Silent Hill's Justifier support is an easter egg, not normal gun gameplay.
  Dragon Quest Monsters contains gun-related code; practical use is unclear.

REGION/PATCH NOTES
  The original USA Resident Evil Survivor release lacks lightgun support.
  Do not select a gun just because a game is a rail shooter.
  If your game has a fan GunCon patch, follow that patch's device instructions.
  In-game calibration may still be needed after selecting the correct type.

WIIMOTE BUTTONS IN THIS BUILD
  Both types: left analog absolute aim; B trigger; A offscreen shot/reload.
  GunCon: 1 = gun A (cover/action in Time Crisis), 2 = gun B.
          + also sends gun B; GunCon has no dedicated Start button.
  Justifier: 1 = auxiliary button; + = Start. 2 is unused.
  Minus and Home are not assigned gun actions.
